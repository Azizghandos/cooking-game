Dapur Kilat is a fast-paced Indonesian warung kitchen game. Customers order nasi goreng, mie goreng, sate ayam, gado-gado and es teh from the order board, and you are the chef: grab raw ingredients from the counters, chop them on the talenan, cook them in the wok, assemble the dish on a plate at the rakit counter, and serve it at the window before the customer's patience runs out. Serve fast for bigger tips, keep a streak going, and level up as the orders come faster — but three failed orders end the shift.

How to play: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve `game/` with any static server). Move with WASD or arrow keys, or click anywhere to walk there. Press E or Space (or click a station up close) to pick up ingredients, place food on the chopping board or in the wok, add prepared food to a plate, and serve a finished dish. P pauses, R restarts after game over, M mutes. All art is drawn on canvas and all sounds are synthesized with WebAudio — the game loads nothing from the network.
